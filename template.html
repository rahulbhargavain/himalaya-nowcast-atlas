What happens when

    Ground truth: airport reports

    METARs from aviationweather.gov: the latest report in the last 3 hours from each airport within about 60 km of mountains over 1,500 m. Hover a station on the map for the raw report. IMD and Nepal DHM automatic stations publish only through their websites and aren't included.

    Rivers: flow against normal

    Modelled discharge from GloFAS v4 (via the Open-Meteo Flood API), not a measured river stage. Grey band: 10th–90th percentile of the same calendar days over the last 15 years; line: last 60 days and 7-day forecast. Lake levels have no free live source, so lakes show extent only.

    snow leopard band

    Modelled habitat, not an observed range map: rugged ground (slope 12° or more) of open alpine types (shrub, meadow, steppe, rock) within the month's elevation band. The band follows wild sheep and ibex, higher in summer and down toward the tree line in winter.
