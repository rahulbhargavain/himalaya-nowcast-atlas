Radar now: the strongest echo within about 3 km on IMD's Mukteshwar radar. Echo arrives: first step of the tracked RainViewer radar with 25 dBZ or more within about 7 km. Rain, temperature, gusts, thunder (forecast thunderstorm hours) and snow line are from the Open-Meteo forecast for each town.
